- **Goes to the line.** When Review points at a line of a change, Diff opens that file, scrolls to the line and marks it, on the old or new side as the comment says.
- **Comment on this line.** Every line of a diff has a comment button, shown on hover, that asks Review to start a comment there.
- **Line numbers lead to the whole file.** Press a line number and Source opens the file at that line, before or after the change.
- **Says when a line is out of view.** If the line pointed at is outside what the diff prints, Diff says so and points you to Source for the whole file.
